# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Netflix IMDb Ratings Hover displays movie and TV ratings while you browse Netflix.

## Information used

When you hover over a Netflix title, the extension reads the title text needed to look up its rating. If you use the popup search, it sends the title you enter for the same purpose. The title is sent to the extension’s rating service, which may query OMDb and other rating sources and cache title and rating results in a shared database. The extension does not attach a name, email address, or account identifier to these title lookups. Service providers may process routine connection and security information when handling requests under their own privacy policies.

The extension stores rating results in Chrome’s local extension storage to make repeat lookups faster. Use **Clear Cache** in the popup to remove this local cache; uninstalling the extension also removes its local data. The shared service cache contains title and rating records and is not associated with an extension user account.

## How information is used and shared

Title text is used only to find and return ratings. Requests are processed by the service hosting provider and database provider, and rating lookups may be sent to OMDb or other rating sources. These providers process information to deliver the requested feature and may handle technical request metadata as described in their privacy policies. We do not sell title lookup data or use it for advertising, analytics, or unrelated profiling.

## Information not collected

The extension does not ask for your name, email, passwords, payment details, or precise location. It does not record your full browsing history, clicks, mouse position, or keystrokes. It reads Netflix title text only to perform the rating lookup described above.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the publisher through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Anant-1/netflix-imdb-rating-extension/issues).
